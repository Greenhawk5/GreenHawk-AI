Story

Old photographs are memories in a language time slowly erases. GreenHawk was built to translate them back — to give history back the color it always had.

Mission

Combine complementary AI models — Zhang, DeOldify, and FLUX — into one studio experience that anyone can use to restore family archives and historical prints.

Why it matters

AI colorization is not just an effect — it is a bridge between generations, an accessible way to keep visual history alive without expert software.

Motivation

Why we built GreenHawk

Three principles that shape every part of the platform.

Preserving History

Old photographs contain irreplaceable memories. GreenHawk helps translate them back into color so they can be shared with new generations.

Accessible AI

Advanced computer vision made available through a simple, elegant interface — no notebooks, no CLI, no configuration required.

Research Driven

A carefully orchestrated pipeline combining Zhang CNN, DeOldify GAN, and FLUX diffusion enhancement — three complementary approaches, one result.
