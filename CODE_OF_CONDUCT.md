# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a friendly, respectful,
and inclusive environment for everyone contributing to GreenHawk AI.

---

# Expected Behavior

Contributors should:

- Be respectful
- Provide constructive feedback
- Focus on technical discussions
- Respect different opinions

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

Examples include:

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Publishing private information
- Malicious actions

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers have the responsibility to clarify standards
and take appropriate actions when necessary.

---

# Contact

For concerns, contact the repository maintainers.
